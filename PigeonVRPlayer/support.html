Playback troubleshooting

  1. Close and reopen PigeonVR Player. If the issue continues, restart your headset.
  2. Check for an app update and a headset software update.
  3. Try another video you have permission to use. If only one video fails, note its file extension and where you opened it from.
  4. If the headset asks for permission when opening media, review the prompt and grant access only if you want the app to use that media.

Compatibility and availability

Once the Meta Horizon Store listing is available, check it for supported headset models and release availability.

Reporting an issue

For app issues, use the developer contact on the Meta Horizon Store listing once it is available.

Include your headset model, app version, what you expected to happen, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Leave out passwords, private media, and other sensitive information.
