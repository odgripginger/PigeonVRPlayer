Data Deletion

Information about app data and deleting support correspondence.

On this page

  • App data
  • Support correspondence
  • After you submit
  • Data held by Meta

App data

PigeonVR Player does not collect or store user data. Rosetta Studios holds no app data to delete.

Support correspondence

If you have contacted Rosetta Studios about PigeonVR Player, reply through the same channel to request deletion of your correspondence. Requests are free and can be made from any region.

Identify the correspondence you want deleted. Leave out passwords, payment details, and private videos.

After you submit a request

Rosetta Studios may ask for information needed to verify that the data belongs to you. If the studio must keep some information to meet a legal obligation, it will explain the reason where it can.

Data held by Meta

For personal data held by Meta in connection with your Meta account, use Meta’s privacy tools. For details about PigeonVR Player, see the Privacy Policy.
