Privacy Policy

How Rosetta Studios handles personal data in connection with PigeonVR Player.

On this page

  • Policy scope
  • App data

Effective November 27, 2026

Who and what this policy covers

Rosetta Studios develops PigeonVR Player, a VR video player intended for distribution through the Meta Horizon Store. This policy addresses the PigeonVR Player app, this website, and communications about the app. Meta’s own privacy terms apply to its handling of data through its platform services.

Data handled by the app

PigeonVR Player does not collect, store, share, or transmit user data. The app processes video as needed for playback on your headset and does not upload your videos or retain your playback history.

Website and support data

This website uses static pages and includes no forms, advertising scripts, or analytics scripts. A hosting provider may process request information such as IP addresses and page requests to serve and secure the site.

If you contact the studio for help, your message may include your email address, device or app version, and any details you choose to share. The studio uses that information to respond and investigate the issue.

Privacy and deletion requests

PigeonVR Player does not collect or store user data, so there is no app data for Rosetta Studios to delete. See Data Deletion for separately held data, such as support correspondence.
